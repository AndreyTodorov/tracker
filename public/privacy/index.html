Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page, your browser sends GitHub’s servers technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the website you came from and your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub processes these logs as its own controller and decides how long it keeps them; I have no access to them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in delivering this website reliably and securely. GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR), and also uses the EU standard contractual clauses. Details are in the GitHub privacy statement and the GitHub Pages documentation.

Your account and portfolio (Google Firebase)

To use the tracker you create an account. Accounts and portfolios are stored with Firebase, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which processes this data on my behalf under the Firebase data processing terms (Art. 28 GDPR).

  • Firebase Authentication stores your email address, your display name and a hash of your password, together with the time of sign-up and last sign-in and the IP address used. Google may process this data on servers in the USA.
  • Firebase Realtime Database, on servers in Belgium (europe-west1), stores:
    • your account record: email address, display name, share code and the portfolios others have shared with you. Only you can read it.
    • your investments: asset, buy price, quantity, amount, currency and dates, with your display name. You can read them, and so can the people you give your share code to. If you list your portfolio on the “Everyone” tab, every signed-in user can see your investments and display name.
    • your share code with your display name, which every signed-in user can look up, so that a code can be resolved to a portfolio.
  • To keep you signed in, Firebase stores a login token in your browser (IndexedDB). This is necessary for the account you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: providing the account and the service you signed up for. Where data is processed in the USA, Google is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses the EU standard contractual clauses. Details are in the Firebase privacy information and the Google privacy policy.

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and everything stored about it at any time: open your profile and choose Delete account.

Prices (CoinGecko)

To show current prices and to find coins when you add an investment, your browser requests them directly from CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com), operated by Gecko Labs Pte. Ltd., Singapore. CoinGecko receives your IP address, the time of the request, the coins and currencies requested and what you type into the coin search, but no account data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in showing up-to-date prices. Singapore has no adequacy decision from the European Commission. Details are in the CoinGecko privacy policy.

Your settings in your browser

The display currency you choose is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key displayCurrency. It stays on your device, is never transmitted, and is stored only to provide the tracker you are using (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). To delete it, clear the stored data for this site in your browser settings.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your email address, your message and the time it was sent in order to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in answering enquiries. Emails are handled by my email provider on my behalf. I delete them when the enquiry is settled, unless the law requires me to keep them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). To use them, write to support@atdrv.eu. Requests about the hosting logs may also need to go to GitHub.

Right to object: you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), as described above for hosting, price lookups and email (Art. 21 GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one in the EU country where you live or work.
